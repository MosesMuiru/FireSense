Signal strength

RSSI in dBm. People and objects cause dips; heat may cause slow drift.

Variability

Spread of the signal over 2 s. Turbulence from a flame raises it.

Flicker spectrum

Last ~25 s. Flames flicker at 2.5–9 Hz (shaded red); walking sits at 0.3–2 Hz (grey).

Link health

Ping replies per second (100 sent). A drop means lost packets.

Flicker f ≈ 1.5/√D Hz for a fire D metres wide: about 6.7 Hz for a 5 cm tray, 4.7 Hz for 10 cm. Signal is sampled 20 times a second, so the spectrum reaches 10 Hz.
